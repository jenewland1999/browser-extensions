Tessera guide

How everything fits together

What is a group?

A group is a container for links and other groups. Use its flow setting to arrange its immediate contents horizontally or vertically.

What is a link?

A link is a tile that opens a web address. It can use the site favicon, an emoji, or an uploaded image.

How deeply can groups nest?

Groups can nest up to levels deep. Collapsing a group from its chevron also collapses every group inside it.

How can I customise things?

Open a group or link to change its name, accent, size, layout, destination, and icon. Page settings control themes, spacing, backgrounds, and the inspector.

Useful behavior

Drag items by their grip to rearrange them. Select mode operates recursively on groups. Hold Shift while dragging the toolbar to snap it to an edge. Lock the layout when you want links to open with one click.

Import and export

Export groups and links, app settings, or both. Import a Tessera or Structured Start Tab JSON file, review its contents, and choose what to bring in before confirming.
